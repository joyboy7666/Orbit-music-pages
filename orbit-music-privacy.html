ORBIT MUSIC

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit Music ("Orbit Music", "Bot", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Bot.

1. Information We Collect

Orbit Music does not intentionally collect, store, or maintain personal data about its users.

Orbit Music does not maintain a database containing Discord usernames, Discord user IDs, Discord passwords, Discord messages, email addresses, IP addresses, payment information, or personal profiles.

2. Music Commands

When you use Orbit Music, the Bot processes commands temporarily in order to provide its functionality. For example, when you request a song, the Bot may temporarily process the requested YouTube URL or search query to play the requested audio. This information is not intentionally stored as a permanent user record.

3. Voice Channels

Orbit Music may connect to Discord voice channels to provide its music functionality. Orbit Music does not record, save, or store voice conversations or user audio.

4. Data Storage

Orbit Music does not maintain persistent user databases or intentionally store personal user information. Temporary information required for active playback, such as the currently playing track, may exist only while the music session is active.

5. Data Sharing

Orbit Music does not sell, rent, or trade user information and does not intentionally share personal user information with third parties. Orbit Music may interact with Discord and YouTube or related services as necessary to provide its functionality. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Deletion

Because Orbit Music does not intentionally maintain a persistent database of user personal information, there is generally no user profile or personal data stored by Orbit Music that requires deletion. Temporary playback information is removed when it is no longer required for the active music session.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to operate Orbit Music securely. However, no online service can guarantee complete security or uninterrupted availability.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy if Orbit Music's functionality or data practices change. The "Last Updated" date will be updated when changes are made.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns regarding Orbit Music, contact the Orbit Music development team through the official support server or support channel.
